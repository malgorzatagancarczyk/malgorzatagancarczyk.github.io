<p class="dl-eyebrow">About</p>
<h1>A highlander from Łącko who reads stories for a living — and for life.</h1>
<p>I am a highlander from Łącko, a village in the Dunajec valley in southern Poland. My family and the generations before them are the root of how I think about identity: who we are, and how the stories we inherit shape us.</p>
<p>I studied Anthropological and Cultural Polish Studies at the Jagiellonian University in Kraków. My work focuses on narratology and processual, narrative concepts of identity — from John Crowley’s <i>Engine Summer</i> to Łukasz Orbitowski’s <i>Kult</i> and the horror film <i>Jennifer’s Body</i>.</p>
<p>Alongside literature, I explore the Lusatian culture and the Bronze Age hillfort at Maszkowice, just next to Łącko.</p>
<p>Outside academia, I work in behavioral growth and marketing automation — currently at User.com. A customer journey is also a story, and people stay with the ones they can recognise themselves in.</p>
<p>My work is listed under <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/publications/">Publications</a>; my background is in my <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/cv/">CV</a>.</p>
