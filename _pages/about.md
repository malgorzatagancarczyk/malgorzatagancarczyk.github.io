I am a highlander from Łącko, a village in the Dunajec valley in southern Poland. My family and the generations before them are the root of how I think about identity: who we are, and how the stories we inherit shape us.

I studied Anthropological and Cultural Polish Studies at the Jagiellonian University in Kraków. My work focuses on narratology and processual, narrative concepts of identity — from John Crowley's *Engine Summer*, the subject of my first academic article, to Łukasz Orbitowski's *Kult* and the horror film *Jennifer's Body*.

Alongside literature, I explore the Lusatian culture and the Bronze Age hillfort at Maszkowice, just next to Łącko.
